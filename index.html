Good things take root.

Morrow House started with a simple thought: bringing a plant home should feel welcoming, not overwhelming. We choose varieties we love, learn how they like to live, and pass that knowledge along with every pot.

Our little studio is built around thoughtful sourcing, useful advice, and making room for the imperfect, rewarding business of keeping things alive.
